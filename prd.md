# PRD: Skyline

Concept: A one-button tower stacking game where every player's best tower becomes a building in a shared class city.

Must-haves:
1. Sign in with Google or as a guest with a name.
2. A crane swings each new floor on a rope. Tap or press Space to release it. It falls with real gravity and lands on the tower. Any part hanging over the edge gets sliced off and falls, so the next floor is smaller. A perfect drop snaps into place at full width. Missing the tower completely ends the run. The score is the number of floors.
3. A shared city skyline shows everyone's best tower as a building, with their name on the roof, and updates live when anyone beats their best.

Screens: Sign In, Play, City
Accounts: Google (Firebase), Vercel
Borrowed from Orbit League: guest names, YOU label, live updates
Later: Profile page with run history, crown for the tallest building, day and night sky, sound effects
